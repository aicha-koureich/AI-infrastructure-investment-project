[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Our revenue is highly dependent on the end-market demand for IT products, and on our partners' strategic initiatives and business models. This end-market demand is influenced by many factors including the introduction of new IT products and software by OEMs, replacement cycles for existing IT products, trends toward cloud computing, seasonality, overall economic growth and general business activity. A difficult and challenging economic environment due to the continued impacts of increased inflation, rising interest rates and Russia's invasion of Ukraine, may also lead to consolidation or decline in the IT distribution industry and increased price-based competition. Our systems design and integration solutions business is highly dependent on the demand for cloud infrastructure, and the number of key customers and suppliers in the market. Our business includes operations in the Americas, Europe and APJ, so we are affected by demand for our products in those regions, and the weakening of local currencies relative to the U.S. Dollar which occurred during fiscal year 2022 may continue to adversely affect the operating results of our Europe and APJ segments.
