[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are carried at cost, less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Depreciation of property and equipment is
computed using the straight-line method over their estimated useful asset lives (30 to 39 years for buildings, 10 years for improvements and three to ten years for equipment, furniture
and software).
