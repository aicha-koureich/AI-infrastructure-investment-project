[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
• Property and equipment assets are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation. Assets placed in service are recorded at cost and depreciated using the straight-line method over the estimated useful life of the asset (12 to 50 years for buildings and 3 to 25 years for equipment), except for leasehold improvements, which are depreciated over the shorter of their economic useful life or their remaining lease term.
