and 2011, respectively, inclusive of rent based on scheduled rent increases and rent holidays recognized on a straight-line basis. Future minimum payments under its various non-cancelable operating
leases in each of the next five years are approximately $15.5 million in 2014, $11.9 million in 2015, $9.6 million in 2016, $7.9 million in 2017, $7.0 million in
2018 and $8.5 million thereafter.
