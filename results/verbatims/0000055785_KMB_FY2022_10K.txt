[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment are stated at cost and are depreciated on the straight-line method. Machinery and equipment are depreciated over their estimated useful lives, primarily ranging from 16 to 20 years. Computer software costs are amortized on the straight-line method over the estimated useful life of the software, which generally does not exceed 5 years.
