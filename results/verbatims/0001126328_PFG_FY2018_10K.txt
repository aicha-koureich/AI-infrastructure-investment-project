[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
We lease buildings and hardware storage equipment under capital leases. As of December 31, 2018 and 2017, these leases had a gross asset
balance of $67.9 million and $60.8 million and accumulated depreciation of $35.3 million and $32.8 million, respectively. Depreciation expense for the years ended
December 31, 2018, 2017 and 2016 was $12.9 million, $13.4 million and $14.8 million, respectively.
