[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment and capitalized computer software are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization. At the time property and equipment are retired or otherwise disposed of, the cost and accumulated depreciation are eliminated from the asset and accumulated depreciation accounts and the profit or loss on such disposition is reflected in income.
