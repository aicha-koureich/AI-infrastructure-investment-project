[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation is provided by the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives, which are 15 to 25 years for buildings
and improvements and 3 to 12 years for machinery and equipment. When property, plant, and equipment are retired, sold,
or otherwise disposed of, the asset’s carrying amount and related accumulated depreciation are removed from the accounts
and any gain or loss is included in operations.
