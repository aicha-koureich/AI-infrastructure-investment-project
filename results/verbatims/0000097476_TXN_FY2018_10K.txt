[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment are stated at cost and depreciated over their estimated useful lives using the straight-line method. Capitalized software licenses generally are amortized on a straight-line basis over the term of the license.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Reflects charges for impacts of accelerated depreciation and changes in exchange rates.
