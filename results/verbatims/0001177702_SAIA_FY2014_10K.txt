[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Maintenance and repairs are charged to operations while replacements and improvements that extend the
asset’s life are capitalized. The Company’s investment in technology equipment and software consists primarily of systems to support customer service and freight management. Depreciation was $58.4 million, $51.0 million and $47.5 million
for the years ended December 31, 2014, 2013 and 2012, respectively. Depreciation and amortization expense includes amortization of assets under capital lease.
