[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
We also offer appliances that integrate our software with hardware and address a wide-range of business needs and use cases, ranging from support for remote or branch offices with limited IT staff up to large corporate data centers. We typically offer appliances via a software only model in which we sell software to a third party, which assembles an integrated appliance that is sold to end user customers. As a result, the revenue and costs associated with hardware are usually not included in our financial statements.  [Source: Item 8]
