[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation and amortization of property, plant and equipment, including equipment
under finance leases (capital leases prior to the adoption of ASC 842), is provided for by the straight-line method over the
estimated useful lives of the respective assets or the lease term, if shorter.
