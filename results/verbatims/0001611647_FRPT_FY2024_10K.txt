[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
The Company recognizes depreciation pursuant to the straight-line method based upon estimated useful lives of 7 years for furniture and office equipment, 9 years for refrigeration equipment, 5 to 10 years for machinery and equipment, and 15 to 39 years for building and improvements.
