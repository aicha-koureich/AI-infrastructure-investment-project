[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Additions and improvements to property and equipment exceeding established minimum amounts with a useful life greater than one year are capitalized at cost. We use the straight-line method of depreciation for buildings, building improvements and equipment. The estimated useful life for buildings and improvements is primarily 15 to 40 years , and for equipment three to 15 years.
