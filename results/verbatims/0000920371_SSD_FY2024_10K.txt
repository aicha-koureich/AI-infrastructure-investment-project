[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalized software projects are amortized over the estimated useful lives of the software.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
The Company has operating leases for certain facilities, equipment, autos and data centers. As an accounting policy for short-term leases, the Company elected to not recognize a right-of-use asset ("ROU asset") and liability if, at the commencement date, the lease (1) has a term of 12 months or less and (2) does not include renewal and purchase options that the Company is reasonably certain to exercise. Monthly payments on short-term leases are recognized on a straight-line basis over the full lease term.  [Source: Item 8]
