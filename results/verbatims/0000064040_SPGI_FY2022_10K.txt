[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalized costs are amortized from the year the software is ready for its intended use over its estimated useful life, three to seven years, using the straight-line method. Capitalized software development and website implementation costs are included in other non-current assets and are presented net of accumulated amortization.
