[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Premises and equipment are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation
and amortization. Estimated useful lives are generally as follows: buildings—39 years; furniture and fixtures—7 years;
computer and communications equipment—3 to 9 years; power plants—15 years; tugs and barges—15 years; and terminals, pipelines and equipment—3 to 25 years. Estimated useful lives for software costs are generally 3 to 5 years.
