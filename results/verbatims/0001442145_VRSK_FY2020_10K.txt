[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
We have operating and finance leases for corporate offices, data centers, and certain equipment that are accounted for under ASC 842. Our leases have remaining lease terms ranging from one year to fourteen years, some of which include the options to extend our leases for up to twenty years, and some of which include the options to terminate our leases within one year. Extension and termination options are considered in our calculation of the right-of-use (“ROU”) assets and lease liabilities when we determine it is reasonably certain that we will exercise those options.
