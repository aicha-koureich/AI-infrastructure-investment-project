[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Fixed assets consist primarily of leasehold improvements, furniture and computer hardware. Such amounts are recorded at cost less
accumulated depreciation and amortization and are included in Other Assets within the accompanying consolidated statements of financial condition. Depreciation and amortization are calculated using
the straight-line method over the assets'
estimated economic useful lives, which for leasehold improvements are the lesser of the lease terms or the life of the asset, and three to seven years for other fixed assets.
