[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Premises and equipment are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization. In general, computer equipment, furniture and equipment and building renovations are depreciated over three , five and ten years, respectively. Software, which includes purchased or externally hosted software is recorded in Other assets and is amortized on a straight-line basis over the lesser of the contract term or estimated useful life of the software.
