[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, equipment and leasehold improvements, which are included in other assets, are stated at cost, less accumulated depreciation. The estimated life is generally 40 years for company occupied real estate property, from one to seven years for leasehold improvements, and from three to seven years for all other property and equipment. Accumulated depreciation of property, equipment and leasehold improvements was $99 million and $97 million at December 31, 2019 and 2018 , respectively.
