[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment is depreciated primarily using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the assets. Computer equipment and purchased software are depreciated over three to seven years .

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
As our business continues to evolve, and in connection with the completion of our strategic initiative to transform our technology infrastructure, we reviewed the remaining estimated useful lives for all of our internally developed software assets during the fourth quarter of 2016.
