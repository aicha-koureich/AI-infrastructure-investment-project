[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Estimated useful lives of three years are used for equipment, software and software development costs, and five years for furniture and fixtures.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
The Company primarily leases facilities for office and data center space under non-cancellable operating leases for its U.S. and international locations that expire at various dates through 2030. For leases with a term greater than 12 months, the Company recognizes a right-of-use asset and a lease liability based on the present value of lease payments over the lease term. Variable lease payments are not included in the lease payments to measure the lease liability and are expensed as incurred.  [Source: Item 8]
