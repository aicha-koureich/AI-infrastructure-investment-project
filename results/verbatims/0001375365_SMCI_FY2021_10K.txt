[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalized software development costs are amortized using the straight-line amortization method over the estimated useful life of the applicable software.

Our solutions include complete servers, storage systems, modular blade servers, blades, workstations, full racks, networking devices, server management software, and server sub-systems.
