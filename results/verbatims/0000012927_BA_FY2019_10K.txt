[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalized internal use software is included in Other assets and amortized using the straight line method over 5 years. Capitalized software as a service is included in Other assets and amortized using the straight line method over the term of the hosting arrangement which is typically no greater than 10 years.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
For operating leases, revenue on leased aircraft and equipment is recorded on a straight-line basis over the term of the lease.
