[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are recorded at cost and d epreciated over their estimated useful lives using the straight-line method. When property and equipment are retired or otherwise disposed of, the cost and accumulated depreciation are removed from our consolidated balance sheets and any resulting gain or loss is reflected in our consolidated statements of income. Generally, estimated useful lives range from three to seven years for equipment, furniture and fixtures.
