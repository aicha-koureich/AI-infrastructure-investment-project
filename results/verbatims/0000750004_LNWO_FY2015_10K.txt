[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
In November 2015, we signed a new five-year contract with the Arizona Lottery to provide an integrated lottery system, including management services for the lottery's central computer systems, software, data center operations, a retailer telecommunications network, retail point-of-sale and player self-service technology and mobile applications. The contract will replace a competitor's system and is effective as of August 31, 2015. The contract includes extension opportunities for up to an additional five years. We expect to begin providing services under the contract on or about August 31, 2016.  [Source: MD&A (Item 7)]
