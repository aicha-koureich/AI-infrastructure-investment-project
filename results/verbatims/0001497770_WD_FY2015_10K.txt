In April 2015, Accounting Standards Update 2015-05 (“ASU 2015-05”), Customer’s Accounting for Fees Paid in a Cloud Computing Arrangement , was issued. ASU 2015-05 provides entities with guidance on determining whether a cloud computing arrangement contains a software license that should be accounted for as internal-use software. ASU 2015-05 is effective for the annual and interim periods beginning January 1, 2016, with early adoption permitted. Entities may select retrospective or prospective adoption of ASU 2015-05. We prospectively adopted ASU 2015-05 in the first quarter of 2016, with no impact to our financial results or operations. ||| Customer’s Accounting for Fees Paid in a Cloud Computing Arrangement ||| , was issued.
