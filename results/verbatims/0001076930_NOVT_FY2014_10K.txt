[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment are recorded at cost, adjusted for any impairment, less accumulated depreciation. Estimated useful lives for buildings and building improvements range from 3 to 30 years and 1 to 10 years for machinery and equipment.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
A hypothetical depreciation of 10% in foreign currency exchange rate, primarily cumulative translation adjustments from the quoted foreign currency exchange rates as of December 31, 2014 would impact shareholder equity by $9.1 million or 4%. We did not hold foreign currency rate derivative contracts as of December 31, 2014.
