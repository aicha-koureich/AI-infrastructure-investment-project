[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant, and equipment are stated at cost and depreciated or depleted over their estimated useful lives, primarily using the straight-line method. The estimated useful lives are: buildings and improvements - 3 to 30 years ; leasehold improvements - the lesser of the term of the lease or 11 years ; machinery and equipment - 2 to 10 years ; and information systems hardware and software - 2 to 5 years .
