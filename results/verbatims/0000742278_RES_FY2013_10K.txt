[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property,
plant and equipment, including software costs, are reported at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization, which is provided
on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful lives of the assets. Annual depreciation and amortization expenses
are computed using the following useful lives: operating equipment, 3 to 20 years; buildings and leasehold improvements, 15 to
39 years; furniture and fixtures, 5 to 7 years; software, 5 years; and vehicles, 3 to 5 years.
