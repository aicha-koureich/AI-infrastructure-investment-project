[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
Costs associated with a cloud computing arrangement (“CCA”), such as software as a service or other hosting arrangement, are capitalized consistent with costs capitalized for internal-use software. If the CCA includes a software license, the software license element of the arrangement is accounted for in the same manner as the acquisition of other software licenses. If the CCA does not include a software license, the service element of the arrangement is accounted for as a service contract. The Company defers certain implementation costs for its CCAs that are service contracts, which are included in prepaids and other current assets and other noncurrent assets in the Company's consolidated balance sheets. The Company amortizes capitalized or deferred implementation costs in a CCA over the life of the service contract.
