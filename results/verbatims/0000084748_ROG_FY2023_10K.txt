[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
We amortize capitalized internal use software costs using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the software, generally from three to five years. Capitalized software is included within the “Property, plant and equipment, net of accumulated depreciation” line item in the consolidated statements of financial position.
