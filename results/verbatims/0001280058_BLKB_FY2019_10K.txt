[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Qualifying capitalized software development costs are amortized on a straight-line basis over the software asset's estimated useful life, which is generally 3 to 7 years . We write off the gross carrying amount and accumulated amortization balances for all fully amortized software development cost assets.
