[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
The Company amortizes capitalized software development costs using the greater of the straight-line amortization method or the ratio that current gross revenues for a
product bear to the total current and anticipated future gross revenues for that product. The estimated useful life for capitalized software for resale is generally three years or less.
