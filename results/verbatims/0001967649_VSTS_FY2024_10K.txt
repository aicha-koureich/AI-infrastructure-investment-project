[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
During fiscal 2022, the Company decided to no longer sell certain personal protective equipment (“PPE”), which required inventory charges to reduce the carrying value of PPE to a zero net realizable value. The Company recorded $26.2 million in inventory charges within “Cost of services provided (exclusive of depreciation and amortization)” in the Combined Statement of Income during fiscal 2022 to reflect the net realizable value of certain PPE inventory. No charges were recorded in fiscal 2023 or 2024 related to PPE.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Property and equipment are stated at cost and are depreciated over their estimated useful lives on a straight-line basis. When a decision has been made to dispose of property and equipment prior to the end of the previously estimated useful life, depreciation estimates are revised to reflect the use of the asset over the shortened estimated useful life. The estimated useful lives for the major categories of property and equipment are 10 to 40 years for buildings and improvements and 3 to 10 years for equipment.
