[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment are recorded at cost, adjusted for any impairment, less accumulated depreciation. Estimated useful lives range from 3 to 30 years for buildings and building improvements and 3 to 10 years for machinery and equipment.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
A hypothetical depreciation of 10% in foreign currency exchange rates from the quoted foreign currency exchange rates as of December 31, 2016 would impact shareholder equity, primarily cumulative translation adjustments, by $7.9 million or 3%. We did not hold foreign currency derivative contracts as of December 31, 2016.
