SECTION TITLE: Note 8. Capitalized Software Development Costs

[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalization ceases and amortization begins no later than the point at which a computer software project is complete and ready for its intended use. Capitalized software costs are amortized over the estimated useful life of the software.
