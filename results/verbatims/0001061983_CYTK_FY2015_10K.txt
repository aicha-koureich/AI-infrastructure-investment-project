[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation and are depreciated on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful lives of the related assets, which are generally three years
for computer equipment and software, five years for laboratory equipment and office equipment, and seven years for furniture and fixtures.
