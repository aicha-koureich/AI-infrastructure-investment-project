[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Buildings and furniture and equipment are initially recorded at cost and are generally depreciated using the straight-line method over estimated useful lives ranging from 10 to 40 years and from 1 to 15 years, respectively. Computer software developed for internal use and for use with the Company’s products is amortized over estimated useful lives ranging from 1 to 15 years using the straight-line method.
