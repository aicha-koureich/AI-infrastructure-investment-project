[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
During 2017, the Company granted executive officers and certain members of management PRSUs if the Company achieved a certain level of adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization ("Adjusted EBITDA") during four consecutive fiscal quarter period ending between (and including) March 31, 2020 and December 31, 2020 (the "Aspirational Plan PRSUs"). On November 16, 2020, the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors determined that the maximum performance condition was achieved. The Aspirational Plan PRSUs vested on December 15, 2020. The Company recorded $49.4 million of stock-based compensation expense related to the Aspirational Plan PRSUs for the year ended December 31, 2020.
