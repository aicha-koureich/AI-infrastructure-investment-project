[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]

positions for more than twelve months. Excluding impairment write downs taken in prior periods, the Company’s assessments indicated that the cause of the market depreciation was primarily the change in interest rates and not the issuer’s
financial condition, or downgrades by rating agencies. In addition, approximately 75.9% of the Company’s investment portfolio matures in five years or less. As a result, the Company has the ability and intent to hold such securities until
maturity.
