[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Capitalized Software Certain direct development costs associated with internal-use software are capitalized within Other assets and amortized over the estimated useful lives of the resulting software. Atleos typically amortizes capitalized internal-use software on a straight-line basis over three to seven years beginning when the asset is substantially ready for use, as this is considered to approximate the usage pattern of the software.
