[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Properties related to gathering and pipeline systems and equipment are depreciated using the
straight-line method based on estimated useful lives ranging from 10 to 25 years. Generally pipeline and transmission systems are depreciated over 12 to 25 years, gathering and
compression equipment is depreciated over 10 years and storage equipment and facilities are depreciated over 10 to 16 years.
