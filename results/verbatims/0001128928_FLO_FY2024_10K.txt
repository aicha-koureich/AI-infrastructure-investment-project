[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
We expect the transformation strategy initiatives will require significant capital investment and expense over the next two years. We currently anticipate the upgrade of our ERP system will cost approximately $350 million (of which approximately 35% has been or is anticipated to be capitalized) and anticipate the upgrade to be completed in Fiscal 2026. As of December 28, 2024, we have incurred costs related to the project of approximately $238 million. In Fiscal 2025, we expect costs for the upgrade of our ERP system (a portion of which may be expensed as incurred, capitalized, recognized as a cloud computing arrangement, or recognized as a prepaid service contract) to be approximately $30 million to $35 million. The increase in costs is due to an increase in the number of planned deployments for Fiscal 2025. Costs related to our digital initiatives are more fluid and cannot currently be estimated. See Item 1A., Risk Factors , “We may experience difficulties in deploying the upgrade of our ERP system.”
