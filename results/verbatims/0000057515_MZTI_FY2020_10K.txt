[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
We capitalize certain costs related to hosting arrangements that are service contracts (cloud computing arrangements). Capitalized costs are included in Other Current Assets or Other Noncurrent Assets and are amortized on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful life. In 2020 and 2019 , we capitalized $10.3 million and $1.7 million , respectively, of deferred software costs related to cloud computing arrangements.
