[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation
for property and equipment is recorded on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful lives of the assets. The estimated useful life for buildings ranges from 10 to 50 years
and five to 20 years for equipment. Equipment includes certain
computer software and software development costs incurred in connection with developing or obtaining software for internal use and is amortized over three to 10 years.
