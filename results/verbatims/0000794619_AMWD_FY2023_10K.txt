[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, Plant and Equipment: Property, plant and equipment is stated on the basis of cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is calculated using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the related assets, which range from 15 to 30 years for buildings and improvements, 3 to 12 years for machinery and equipment, and 3 to 10 years for software.
