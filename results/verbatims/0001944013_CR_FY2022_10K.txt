[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment is stated at cost and depreciation is calculated by the straight-line method over the estimated useful
lives of the respective assets, which range from 10 to 25 years for buildings and improvements and three to 10 years for machinery and equipment.
