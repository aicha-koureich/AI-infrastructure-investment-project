[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Buildings, furniture, fixtures, leasehold improvements and equipment are carried at cost, less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Capital leases, where the Company is the lessee, are included in premises and equipment at the capitalized amount less accumulated amortization. The Company primarily uses the straight-line method of depreciation over the estimated useful lives of the assets, which range from 10 years to 40 years for buildings, and two years to 15 years for leasehold improvements, and for furniture, fixtures and equipment.
