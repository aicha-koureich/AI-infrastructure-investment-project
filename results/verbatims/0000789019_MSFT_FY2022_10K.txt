[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
In July 2022, we completed an assessment of the useful lives of our server and network equipment. Due to investments in software that increased efficiencies in how we operate our server and network equipment, as well as advances in technology, we determined we should increase the estimated useful lives of both server and network equipment from four years to six years. We had previously increased the estimated useful lives of both server and network equipment in July 2020.
