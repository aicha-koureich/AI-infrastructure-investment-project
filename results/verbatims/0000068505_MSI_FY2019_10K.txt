[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
On March 13, 2017, the Company completed the acquisition of Interexport, a managed service provider for communications systems to public safety and commercial customers in Chile, for a purchase price of $98 billion Chilean pesos, or approximately $147 million U.S. dollars based on cash payments of $55 million , net of cash acquired, and assumed liabilities of $92 million , primarily related to capital leases. As a result of the acquisition, the Company recognized $61 million of identifiable intangible assets, $70 million of acquired property, plant and equipment and $16 million of net other tangible assets. The estimated identifiable intangible assets were classified as $56 million of customer-related intangibles and $5 million of other intangibles and will be amortized over a period of seven years . The business is part of the Software and Services segment.
