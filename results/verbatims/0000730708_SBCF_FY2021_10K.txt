[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Bank Premises and Equipment: Bank premises and equipment are stated at cost, less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Depreciation and amortization are recognized principally by the straight-line method, over the estimated useful lives as follows: buildings - 25-40 years, leasehold improvements 5-25 years, furniture and equipment - 3-12 years.
