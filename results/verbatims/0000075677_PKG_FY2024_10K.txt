[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
When property and equipment are retired, sold, or otherwise disposed of, the asset’s carrying amount and related accumulated depreciation are removed from the accounts and any gain or loss is included in “Other expense, net” in our Consolidated Statements of Income.

Not disclosed

[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
