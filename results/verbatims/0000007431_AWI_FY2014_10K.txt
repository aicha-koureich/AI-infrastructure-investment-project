[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property plant and equipment is recorded at cost reduced by accumulated depreciation. Machinery and equipment includes manufacturing equipment (depreciated over 3 to 15 years), computer equipment (depreciated over 3 to 5 years) and office furniture and equipment
(depreciated over 5 to 7 years). Within manufacturing equipment, assets that are subject to quick obsolescence or wear out quickly, such as tooling and engraving equipment, are depreciated over shorter periods (3 to 7 years). Heavy production
equipment, such as conveyors and production presses, are depreciated over longer periods (10 to 15 years). Computer software is depreciated
over 3 to 7 years.
