[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
These costs are amortized over the expected useful life of the software- generally 3 to 5 years once the assets are ready for their intended use.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Depreciation is computed using the straight-line method on an individual asset basis over the following estimated useful lives: buildings and improvements, 10 to 33 years (for finance leases, the remaining term of the lease); and machinery and equipment, 4 to 10 years (for financing leases, the remaining term of the lease).

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Not disclosed

[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
The Company capitalizes costs incurred with the implementation of a cloud computing arrangement that is a service contract, consistent with its policy for software for internal use. The capitalized costs are reflected in "Other non-current assets" on its Consolidated Balance Sheets and expensed over the term of the related hosting arrangement.
