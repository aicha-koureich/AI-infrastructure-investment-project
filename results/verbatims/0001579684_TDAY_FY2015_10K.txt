[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation is calculated under the
straight-line method over the estimated useful lives, principally 3 to 40 years for buildings and improvements, 1 to 20 years for machinery and equipment, and 1 to 10 years for furniture, fixtures and computer software.
