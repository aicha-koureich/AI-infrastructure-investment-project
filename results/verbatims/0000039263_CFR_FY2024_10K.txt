[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
Technology, Furniture and Equipment. Technology, furniture and equipment expense for 2024 increased $13.2 million, or 9.8%, compared to 2023. The increase was primarily related to increases in cloud services expense (up $9.2 million), service contracts expense (up $3.4 million), software amortization (up $1.2 million), and software maintenance expense (up $1.2 million), among other things.
