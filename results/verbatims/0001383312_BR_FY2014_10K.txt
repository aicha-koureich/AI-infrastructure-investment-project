[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
I. Deferred Data Center Costs. Data center costs relate to conversion costs
associated with our principal data center systems and applications. Costs directly related to the activities necessary to make the data center usable for its intended purpose are deferred and amortized over the life of the contract on a
straight-line basis commencing on the date the data center has achieved full functionality. These deferred costs are reflected in Other non-current assets in the Consolidated Balance Sheets at June 30, 2014 and 2013, respectively. See
Note 11, “Other non-current assets”.  [Source: Item 8]
