[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Premises and equipment are carried at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Estimated lives range up to 40 years for buildings, up to 12 years for furniture and equipment, and the shorter of lease term or estimated useful life for leasehold improvements.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Noninterest expense decreased $1.5 billion to $2.6 billion primarily due to lower non-core mortgage costs and reduced operational costs from the sale of the non-U.S. consumer credit card business. Also, the prior-year period included a $316 million impairment charge related to certain data centers.  [Source: MD&A (Item 7)]
