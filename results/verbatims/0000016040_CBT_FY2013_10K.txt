[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
In the third quarter of fiscal 2013, the Company changed the estimated
depreciable lives of certain machinery and equipment from ten to twenty-five years to reflect the Company’s experience. The change was accounted for as a change in estimate. Based on the asset base at the time of the change, the Company expects
the change will increase Income from continuing operations by $9 million ($0.14 per diluted common share) over a twelve month period. The change increased Income from continuing operations by $4 million ($0.06 per diluted common share) in fiscal
2013.
