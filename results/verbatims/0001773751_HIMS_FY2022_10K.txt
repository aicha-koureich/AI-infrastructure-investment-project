[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are recorded at cost, less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Property and equipment are depreciated or amortized using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives ranging from two to five years and consist primarily of facility equipment, computers, equipment, furniture, and fixtures.
