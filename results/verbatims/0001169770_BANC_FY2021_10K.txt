[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Premises and equipment are recorded at cost less accumulated depreciation. The straight-line method is used for depreciation with the following estimated useful lives: building - 40 years and leasehold improvements - shorter of useful life or life of lease, and furniture, fixtures, and equipment - 3 to 7 years.
