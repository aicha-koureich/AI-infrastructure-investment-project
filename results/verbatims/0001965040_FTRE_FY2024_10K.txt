[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
Cloud Computing Arrangements ||| The Company defers costs incurred with the implementation of a cloud computing arrangement that is a service contract, consistent with its policy for software developed or obtained for internal use. Deferred cloud computing arrangement implementation costs are amortized using the straight-line method over the remaining term of the related hosting contract. As of December 31, 2024 the Company has a current asset of $2.5 included in prepaid expenses and other and a non-current asset of $18.7 included in other assets, net in the consolidated balance sheets that have been deferred in conjunction with implementations. There were no deferred costs for cloud computing arrangements as of December 31, 2023.
