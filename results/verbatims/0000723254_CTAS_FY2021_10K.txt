Amortization of internal use software begins when the software is ready for service and continues on the straight-line method over the estimated useful life, generally 10 years. We recorded amortization expense related to internal use software of $22.3 million, $21.5 million and $21.6 million for the fiscal years ended May 31, 2021, 2020 and 2019, respectively.
