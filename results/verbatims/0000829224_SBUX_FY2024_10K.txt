[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment is carried at cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is computed using the straight-line method over estimated useful lives of the assets, generally ranging from 2 to 15 years for equipment, 30 to 40 years for buildings, and 2 to 8 years for capitalized software.
