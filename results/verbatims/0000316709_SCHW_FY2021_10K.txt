[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Equipment, office facilities, and property are recorded at cost net of accumulated depreciation and amortization, except for land, which is recorded at cost. Equipment, office facilities, and property (other than land) are depreciated on a straight-line basis over their estimated useful lives.
