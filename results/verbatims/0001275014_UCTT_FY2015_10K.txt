[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Equipment and Leasehold Improvements — Equipment and leasehold improvements are
stated at cost, or, in the case of equipment under capital leases, the present value of future minimum lease payments at inception of the related lease. Depreciation and amortization are computed using the straight-line method over the lesser of the
estimated useful lives of the assets or the terms of the leases. Useful lives range from three to fifteen years.
