[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Property, Plant and Equipment Property, plant and equipment (“PP&E”) is carried at cost and includes expenditures for new facilities and equipment and those costs which substantially increase the useful lives or capacity of existing PP&E. Factory machinery and equipment is depreciated over periods ranging from 5 to 25 years with the majority of such assets (principally glass-melting furnaces and forming machines) depreciated over 7 to 15 years . Buildings and building equipment are depreciated over periods ranging from 10 to 50 years.
