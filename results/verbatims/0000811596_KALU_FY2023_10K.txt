Cloud Computing Implementation Costs. We defer implementation costs associated with a software hosting arrangement that meets the definition of a service contract. When the project is placed into service, we amortize the deferred implementation costs over the term of the hosting arrangement inclusive of expected renewal periods to the same line item in the Statements of Consolidated Income (Loss) as the underlying arrangement.
