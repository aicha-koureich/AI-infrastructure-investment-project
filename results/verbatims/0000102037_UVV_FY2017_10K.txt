[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
In April 2015, the FASB issued Accounting Standards Update 2015-05, “Intangibles - Goodwill and Other - Internal-Use Software - Customer’s Accounting for Fees Paid in a Cloud Computing Arrangement” (“ASU 2015-05”). ASU 2015-05 requires customers who enter into a cloud computing arrangement that includes a software license to account for the arrangement as an intangible asset. If the cloud computing arrangement does not include a software license, the arrangement is accounted for as a service contract.
