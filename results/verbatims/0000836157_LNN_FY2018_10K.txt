[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Provisions for depreciation and amortization have been computed principally on the straight-line method for property, plant, and equipment. The Company also markets patented technology under the FieldNET Advisor ™ product name which delivers information that helps farmers decide precisely when, where and how much to irrigate.
