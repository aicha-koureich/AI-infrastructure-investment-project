[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment, net is recorded at cost and is depreciated using the straight-line method. Useful lives for financial reporting range from 3 to 10 years for machinery and equipment and 10 to 45 years for buildings. Capitalized software costs are amortized over the estimated useful lives of the software, which range from 3 to 10 years.
