[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
The estimated useful lives of property and equipment range from 1 to 10 years. Fixtures, which are comprised primarily of leasehold improvements and equipment under finance leases, are amortized on a straight-line basis over their estimated useful lives or for leasehold improvements, the related lease term, if less. The estimated useful life of costs capitalized is evaluated for each specific project and ranges from 1 to 5 years (see Note 7 - Property and Equipment).
