[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are recorded at historical cost, less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Equipment is depreciated over the estimated useful lives of the assets, while leasehold improvements are amortized over the lesser of the estimated economic useful life of the
asset or the term of the lease. Computer equipment is depreciated over three to five years and office furniture and equipment are depreciated over five to seven years. Qualifying costs for internally
developed software are capitalized and amortized over the expected useful life of the developed software, not to exceed three years.
