[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
The $1.1 billion increase in net sales in 2021 compared to 2020 was due to higher sales across all end markets. Sales in the communications end market represented 42% of the increase, driven primarily by the further adoption of 5G smartphones. The automotive and industrial end market recovered in the current year from weakened demand relating to the Covid-19 pandemic in the prior year, representing 25% of our growth in net sales. Sales in the computing end market accounted for 20% of the increase in 2021 due to growth in all applications, primarily due to the shift in cloud computing needs and new product introductions within personal computing. The consumer end market continues to see strong sales, primarily due to demand for both home and personal electronics, and made up the remaining 13% of the increase compared to 2020.
