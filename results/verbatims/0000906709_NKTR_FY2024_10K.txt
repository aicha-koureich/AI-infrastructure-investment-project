Interest expense on liabilities related to the sales of future royalties is imputed on the unamortized portion using the effective interest method. We periodically assess the estimated royalty payments under our licensees, and, as the amount or timing of such payments of these estimates change, we prospectively adjust the imputed interest rate and the related amortization of the respective liability. The actual interest rate will be affected by the timing of the royalty payments and changes in the forecasted revenue.
