[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation of property, plant and equipment is provided on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful lives of the assets. For assets classified as machinery and equipment, lives generally used for calculating depreciation expense range from 10 to 30 years for manufacturing equipment, five to 10 years for furniture and fixtures, three to five years for vehicles and three to 10 years for computer equipment and software.
