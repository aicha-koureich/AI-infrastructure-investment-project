[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Amortization of capitalized software costs is calculated by the greater of (a) the ratio that current gross revenues for a product bear to the total of current and anticipated future gross revenues for that product or (b) the straight-line method over the remaining estimated economic life of the product, including the period being reported on, typically two to five years. Amortization expense related to capitalized software development costs was $5.4 million, $3.5 million, and $2.4 million for each of the years ended December 31, 2017, 2016, and 2015, respectively.
