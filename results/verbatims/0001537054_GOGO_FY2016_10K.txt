[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and Equipment and Depreciation— Property and equipment, including leasehold improvements, are stated at historical cost,
less accumulated depreciation. Network asset inventory and construction in progress, which include materials, transmission and related equipment, and interest and other costs relating to the construction and development of our network, are not
depreciated until they are put into service.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Due to advances in technology and changes in agreements with our airline partners, with
respect to upgrading equipment, we periodically reassess the useful lives of our property and equipment.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Cost of service revenue for the CA-NA and CA-ROW segments
includes network related expenses (satellite network expenses, including costs for transponder capacity and backhaul, as well as data centers, network operations center, network technical support), aircraft operations, component assembly, portal
maintenance, revenue share and transactional costs.  [Source: MD&A (Item 7)]
