[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Buildings, furniture and fixtures, and equipment
are carried at cost less accumulated depreciation. Buildings and related components are depreciated using the straight-line method
using a useful life of fifty years for buildings and a range of two to ten years for equipment, computer hardware and software,
and furniture and fixtures.
