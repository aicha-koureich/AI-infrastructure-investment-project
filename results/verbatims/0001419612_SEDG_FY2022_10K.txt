[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
The Company incurs costs to implement
cloud computing arrangements ("CCA") that are hosted by third party vendors. Implementation costs associated with CCA are capitalized
when incurred during the application development phase until the software is ready for its intended use. The costs are then amortized
on a straight-line basis over the contractual term of the cloud computing arrangement and are recognized as an operating expense within
the consolidated statements of income. Capitalized amounts related to such arrangements are recorded within other long-term assets in
the consolidated balance sheets. Cash payments for CCA implementation costs are classified as cash outflows from operating activities.
