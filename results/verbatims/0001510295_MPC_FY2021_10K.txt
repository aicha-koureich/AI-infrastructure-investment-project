[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, plant and equipment are recorded at cost and depreciated on a straight-line basis over the estimated useful lives of the assets, generally ten to 40 years for refining and midstream assets, 25 years for office buildings and four to seven years for other miscellaneous fixed assets.
