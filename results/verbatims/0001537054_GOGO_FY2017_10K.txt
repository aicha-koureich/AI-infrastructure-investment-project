[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Under agreements with certain of our airline partners to upgrade our equipment on certain aircraft or decommission certain aircraft on which
our equipment is installed, on a quarterly basis we reassess the useful life of the affected equipment. As a result, we shorten the useful lives of the affected equipment to be consistent with the estimated upgrade date or aircraft decommissioning
date, as applicable. We also shorten the related remaining amortization period for deferred airborne lease incentives for certain equipment on aircraft to be decommissioned.
