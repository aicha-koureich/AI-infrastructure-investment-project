[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property and equipment are stated at cost and depreciated over the estimated useful lives of the assets using the straight-line method. Equipment is depreciated over an average estimated useful life of three to seven years.

In the ordinary course of our business, we collect and store confidential and sensitive electronic information on our networks and in our data centers.
