[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
In the case of plant and equipment, the historical cost is depreciated on a straight-line basis over its estimated economic useful life.

[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
Expenditures for maintenance and repairs are charged to expense as incurred, whereas major improvements that increase asset values and extend useful lives are capitalized.
