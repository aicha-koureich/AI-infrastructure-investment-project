[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Depreciation of property, plant and equipment generally is computed using the straight-line method based on the estimated useful lives of the assets. The estimated useful lives of machinery and equipment primarily range from three to fifteen years, with the majority in the range of five to ten years.
