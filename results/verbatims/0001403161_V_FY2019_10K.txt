[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Property, equipment and technology are recorded at historical cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization, which are computed on a straight-line basis over the asset’s estimated useful life. Depreciation and amortization of technology, furniture, fixtures and equipment are computed over estimated useful lives ranging from 2 to 10 years. Fully depreciated assets are retained in property, equipment and technology, net, until removed from service.
