Our services offerings, which we consider to be part of our growth portfolio, include wireless product design and development services, CRM consulting services, application development services, licenses to use The Social Machine® application for use on the Force.com platform, our PAAS recurring revenue generated from Device Cloud platform, and post-contract customer support and fees associated with technical support and training. No revenue was recorded in fiscal 2013 for licenses to use The Social Machine® application as we won our first contract late in fiscal 2013.
