[VERBATIM — Durée de vie]
Premises, equipment, capital leases and leasehold improvements are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation and amortization. Capitalized leases are amortized using the same methods as premises and equipment over the estimated useful lives or lease terms, whichever is less.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Not disclosed

[VERBATIM — Segments (Task 6)]
