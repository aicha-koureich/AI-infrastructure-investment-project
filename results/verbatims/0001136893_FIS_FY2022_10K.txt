[VERBATIM — Changement de politique]
During the years ended December 31, 2022 and 2021, the Company recorded $32 million and $144 million, respectively, of software asset impairments and $156 million and $145 million, respectively, of incremental software amortization expense driven by the Company's platform modernization.

[VERBATIM — Infrastructure IA]
Following the successful modernization of our IT infrastructure and consolidation of our data centers, we are now accelerating the modernization of our strategic applications and sunsetting of our redundant platforms. Our multi-year platform modernization initiative is designed to create a componentized, cloud-native set of capabilities that can be consumed by clients as end-to-end business applications or as individual components. Although our platform modernization has resulted and will continue to result in additional near-term costs, we expect it will continue to result in improvements in our operational efficiencies over time.
